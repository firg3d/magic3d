The 3D Academy — everything you need to print well

Four chapters written in a workshop with 20 printers running every day, not translated from a manual. No account, no subscription, no email asked for at the door. Read what you need, when you need it.

Who wrote this

The 3D Academy is written by the team at Firg3D Printing SRL, the workshop in District 5, Bucharest, which runs the Praktik3D shop and the Magic3D platform. We have printed commercially since 2022, with 20 FDM printers, SLA resin printing, 3D scanning and laser engraving, for orders from a single piece to a few hundred.

Everything you read here comes from real orders, not from manufacturer documentation. When an observation only holds under certain conditions, we say so.

Where to start

Do not read it end to end if you already have a problem. Pick by where you are:

You have a printer and the print has just failed → start with chapter 1. That is where the seven defects that keep coming back are, recognised by how the part looks.

You have a printer and do not know which material to buy → chapter 2. One question settles it: where the object ends up.

You do not have a printer yet → chapter 3. Three categories, not twenty models.

You already print and want to sell → chapter 4. What changes when you go from one piece to fifty, and how to price so you are not working for free.

The four chapters

1. The first print that fails, and why

Adhesion, lifted corners, split layers, strings between parts, a splayed base, walls with gaps, shifted layers. Every defect with its real cause and its fix, plus the rule that saves the most hours: change one thing at a time. 9 minutes

2. Filament: what to choose and what for

PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU — judged by where the object ends up, not by tables of properties. Why a PLA keychain left on a car dashboard warps, what survives the sun, and why moisture in the filament ruins more prints than any wrong setting. 8 minutes

3. Which 3D printer to buy Coming soon

Three categories: your first printer, the printer for someone who wants to sell, and multicolour. What actually matters when choosing, what multicolour costs in wasted material, and why two mid-range machines beat one expensive one. 8 minutes

4. From one piece to an order Coming soon

What changes when you print fifty: the layout on the plate, what wears out in a batch, the five components of the real price, and where the time in a personalised order actually goes. 9 minutes

Fifteen questions, five minutes. Every wrong answer comes with the explanation and a link to the right chapter. Nothing is saved, nothing is asked of you.
