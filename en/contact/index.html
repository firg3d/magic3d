The platform grows out of what you tell us. The messages below reach the workshop directly, and the ones with an email address get an answer.

What helps us most

  • Problems you hitWhat you tried to do and what happened instead. One screenshot saves us ten questions.
  • Themes and decorationsA model you need that is not in the library. If you have a photo of the object, attach it.
  • Generators we are missingA product you print often and would like to configure by itself, the way letters or keychains do.
  • Bases and standsStand shapes you use on orders and that we could add to the library.
  • The commercial licenceAny question about what you may sell and how to get the token.
