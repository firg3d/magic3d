What you want to know before you start

What does Magic3D produce?

Print-ready 3MF files, with the parts already oriented for the printer and laid out on beds. Alongside each export you get a production sheet in plain text, with the colour table, the assembly steps and a checklist to run before delivery.

Do I need to know 3D modelling?

No. You pick the letter, the font and the dimensions from panels, and the geometry generates itself. There are no sketches, constraints or feature trees like in a CAD program.

When do I need a commercial licence?

When you sell what you make with the app — prints, physical objects or files. For your own projects, for family or as gifts, the app is free and complete. The licence does not unlock features; it grants the right to sell and gives you a licence number that appears on the production sheet.

What is the supporter token?

It is a way to back the development, not a licence. You get the token3d files to print yourself and a badge you can show in your renders. It does NOT grant commercial use: you stay in personal-use mode.

Which printer does it work with?

Any FDM printer. The export is standard 3MF, opened by Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer and Cura. You choose the bed size in the app, and parts that don't fit are split across several beds automatically.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All generation runs locally, on your own computer. Nothing is sent to any server and you don't need a connection to work or to export.

What is the Praktik3D signature?

A small engraved marking reading praktik3d.ro, on the new generators — not on the letter ones, which stay free of any marking. It is how we sign our work on a free export. With the commercial licence you replace it with your own logo, or remove it.
