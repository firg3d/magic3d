## Opening the generators

A generator builds a finished part from a few values: you type the text or the size, the part appears on the plate and you can change it freely in the editor afterwards, like any other shape.

Open them with {{m3d:gen.buton}} in the title row of the editor, or pick one in the numbered catalog of the start page: the start page opens a new design with that generator already open. The panel shows the part on the plate while you change the values; {{m3d:gen.adauga}} keeps it.

## Letters, names and keyrings

These make parts from a text and a font:

- {{m3d:comutator.litereDecor}}, {{m3d:comutator.nume3d}} and {{m3d:comutator.cuvinte}} - standing letters and words, joined so they print as one piece;
- {{m3d:comutator.brelocuri}}, {{m3d:comutator.articulat}} and {{m3d:comutator.brelocDecor}} - keyrings with a ring hole, also with moving parts;
- {{m3d:comutator.fagure}}, {{m3d:comutator.led}}, {{m3d:comutator.numeLed}} and {{m3d:comutator.cutii}} - honeycomb letters, letters with a channel for an LED strip, letter boxes;
- {{m3d:comutator.placute}}, {{m3d:comutator.rame}} and {{m3d:comutator.huse}} - tags, themed frames and phone cases.

When the font lacks a letter (a diacritic, say), the panel says so instead of leaving a gap.

## Stamps, embossers and ornaments

Under {{m3d:gen.personalizate}}:

- {{m3d:comutator.stampila}} - a text or a logo from a picture, for ink or for clay and fondant, with the handle you choose;
- {{m3d:comutator.presa}} - a raised emblem on paper and card: the clamp prints with its hinge already assembled;
- {{m3d:comutator.ornament}} - a disc with a name or a bauble in two halves, also as a pack for the whole family.

A logo comes from a PNG, JPG or SVG: the panel traces its outline. Only upload pictures you have the right to use.

## Molds

Under {{m3d:gen.matrite}}:

- {{m3d:comutator.tava}} - shapes in a grid for soap, wax, plaster or resin, or a master for a silicone mold, with the volume worked out;
- {{m3d:comutator.silicon}} - upload a model (STL, OBJ, 3MF) or take the object selected in the editor: you get the mold box, the keyed parting plate, the pour channel and how much silicone it takes;
- {{m3d:comutator.presalut}} - two halves with an overflow channel and pins, or a large soap stamp.

## Editing a generated part again

A part made by a generator remembers its values. Select it and press {{m3d:gen.editeaza}}: the panel opens with the values it was made with, and the part is rebuilt where it stands. Once you change the part's shape by hand in the editor, it is an ordinary body.

## Ordering a print

No printer? {{m3d:oferta.buton}} in the title row prepares the files (3MF, STL and a picture), shows an estimate and checks that the part is closed and fits the workshop's printer. Fill in your name, phone and city, tick {{m3d:oferta.student}} if it applies, and the files go to the workshop with a WhatsApp message already written. The link to the files expires after 20 days; the form data is not stored on the server.
